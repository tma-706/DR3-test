============================================================
NEO-VERIDIA WASTE COLLECTION SCHEDULE - 2026
ZONE: A (Downtown / Central District)
VALIDITY: February 1, 2026 - December 31, 2026
============================================================

IMPORTANT: 
- Bins must be out by 8:00 AM. 
- No collection on Public Holidays.

------------------------------------------------------------
WEEKLY ROUTINE (Standard Waste)
------------------------------------------------------------

MONDAY:
  - GREEN BIN (Kitchen Waste)
  - GREY BIN (Residual Waste)

TUESDAY:
  - NO COLLECTION

WEDNESDAY:
  - GREEN BIN (Kitchen Waste)
  - BLUE BIN (Recyclables - Paper/Glass/Plastic)

THURSDAY:
  - NO COLLECTION

FRIDAY:
  - GREEN BIN (Kitchen Waste)
  - GREY BIN (Residual Waste)

SATURDAY & SUNDAY:
  - NO COLLECTION (Drop-off centers open 9am-4pm)

------------------------------------------------------------
SPECIAL COLLECTION (Hazardous Waste - RED BIN)
------------------------------------------------------------

Hazardous waste is ONLY collected on the FIRST WEDNESDAY of 
each month alongside the standard Wednesday pickup.

2026 RED BIN DATES (Zone A):
- Feb 4, Mar 4, Apr 1, May 6, Jun 3
- Jul 1, Aug 5, Sep 2, Oct 7, Nov 4, Dec 2
